Questions

Asked, answered.

I don't have much footage. Will this work?

Yes. Most businesses have more than they think. We'll send a shot list of ten quick clips you can film on your phone in about ten minutes.

Do you post the videos for us?

We deliver finished videos with captions, hashtags and a suggested schedule, so posting takes seconds. Growth clients get copies tuned for each platform.

Can you guarantee more customers or views?

No one honestly can. What we guarantee is the work: the number of videos, the quality and on-time delivery. Consistent, well-edited video gives you the best chance.

Who owns the videos?

You do. Your footage and finished videos are yours. We never share or post them anywhere without your written permission.

How do I pay?

Through a secure Stripe payment link, by card or US bank transfer. We never see or store your card number.

Where are you based?

We're an online studio based in Wichita, Kansas, working with businesses across the metro and anywhere in the US. Everything happens by email and shared folders.
