Ask about it

Questions

Straight answers.

I don't have much footage. Will this work?

Yes. Most businesses have more than they think. We'll send a shot list of 10 quick clips you can film on your phone in about 10 minutes.

Do you post the videos for us?

We deliver finished videos with captions, hashtags and a suggested schedule so you can post in seconds. Growth clients get copies tuned for each platform.

Can you guarantee more customers or views?

No one honestly can. What we guarantee is the work: the number of videos, the quality, and on-time delivery. Consistent, well-edited video is what gives you the best chance.

Who owns the videos?

You do. Your footage and finished videos are yours. We never share or post them anywhere without your written permission.

How do I pay?

Through a secure Stripe payment link, by card or US bank transfer. We never see or store your card number.

Are you local?

Yes, we're based in Wichita, Kansas, and work with businesses across the metro, plus remote clients anywhere in the US.

Policies

Refunds and cancellations

  • Every new client can get a free sample edit before paying anything.
  • Monthly plans are billed in advance. Cancel anytime by email before your next billing date and you won't be charged again.
  • Monthly plans: if we haven't started work on your month's videos, you can request a full refund for that month within 7 days of payment.
  • Monthly plans: if your first batch isn't delivered within 5 business days of us receiving your footage, you can request a full refund for that month.
  • Founding 90-day plan: this discounted prepaid plan is non-refundable once paid. Please try the free sample edit first so you know what you're getting.
  • Once a month's videos have been delivered, that month isn't refundable, but each video includes a round of revisions until it's right.

Privacy
