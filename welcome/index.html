Common questions

Before you begin.

What should I film?

Your work in action, before and after results, your team, quick tips for customers, a short tour, a customer saying something kind (with their permission). Clips of 10 to 60 seconds are perfect. Talking to the camera works well; we add the captions.

Vertical or horizontal?

Vertical is best, but horizontal works too. We fit it to Reels, TikTok and Shorts without cutting off what matters.

Do I need good lighting or a fancy camera?

No. A phone is enough. Face a window for soft light and get close for clear sound. We fix levels, cut the dead air and add captions.

How big can files be?

Large files are fine. Upload the originals. If an upload stops, open the link again and re-send that clip.

Can I send old videos or photos?

Yes. Old videos are great material. Photos can be used as quick slides inside a video.

Do you post the videos for me?

We deliver finished videos with captions, hashtags and a posting schedule so you can post in seconds. Growth plans include versions tuned for each platform.

Can I use music?

Add a trending sound from the app's own music library when you post. That's licensed for you by the platform. We keep the voice clear so any sound fits underneath.

How do revisions work?

Reply to the delivery email with the video number and what to change. Each video includes one round of revisions.

How do I cancel or change plans?

Email us before your next billing date and you won't be charged again. The Founding 90-day plan is a prepaid, non-refundable rate. See the refund policy.

Who owns the videos?

You do. We never post or share your footage anywhere without your written permission.
