Install

  1. Open Magnificent-Media-Photo-Studio.dmg from your Downloads folder.
  2. Drag Photo Studio Direct into your Applications folder.
  3. Open it from Applications. The one-minute tour shows you around, and Help › Open Sample Photos gives you seven photos to practice on.

Unlock with your license key

  1. Find the key in your email from hello@magnificentmediaict.com.
  2. In Photo Studio, click any Unlock with Pro button.
  3. Paste the key and click Activate. It works offline.

One license covers 2 of your Macs: enter the same key on each.

Lost your key? Email hello@magnificentmediaict.com from your order email.

Updates

Every version 1 update (1.1, 1.2 and on) is free with your license; version 2.0 will be a $25 upgrade for license holders. Photo Studio checks this site once a day and tells you when a new version is ready. Download it here and drag it into Applications to replace the old one. Your edits and your key stay in place. Untick "Check for updates automatically" in that window to turn the check off; Photo Studio Direct › Check for Updates… still works any time.
