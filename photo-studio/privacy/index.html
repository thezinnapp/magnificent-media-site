Magnificent Media Photo Studio

Privacy, plainly.

Effective November 3, 2026

Photo Studio collects no data. All editing, including the AI features, happens on your Mac. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not send your photos or any information about you to us or to anyone else.

Your photos

  • The app opens only the photos and folders you choose or drag in. Your originals are never changed.
  • Your edits are saved as small recipe files inside the app's own storage on your Mac, so you can come back to them. Deleting the app removes them.
  • Exports are saved only where you choose. By default, location (GPS) data is removed from exported photos. You can turn this off in the Export window.

AI features

Enhance, subject, people, background and sky masks, Skin Guard, Skin Smooth and Background Blur use Apple's on-device frameworks (Vision and Core Image). The analysis runs on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded.

Enhance in My Style remembers how you edited your photos (a compact numeric fingerprint of each photo plus your slider changes) so it can suggest your look for new photos. This memory is stored only on your Mac, is never sent anywhere, and can be erased at any time with the "Forget" button in the Enhance panel.

Purchases

In the Mac App Store edition, Photo Studio Pro is a subscription processed by Apple. We never see your card or account details.

If you buy a lifetime license on our website, Stripe processes the payment. We keep your email address and order number so we can send your license key and help you with support. The app checks the key offline and stores it only on your Mac.

Children

Update check (website edition only)

The edition sold on our website checks for a newer version once a day by downloading a small file from magnificentmediaict.com. The request contains no photos and no personal information; like any web request, it shows your IP address to our web host. You can turn automatic checks off in the update window. The Mac App Store edition makes no such request; the App Store handles its updates.

Editing in Apple Photos

When you edit with Photo Studio inside the Photos app, the photo is processed on your Mac exactly like in the app. Your edit settings are stored with the photo in your Photos library, so you can change them later. Nothing is sent to us.

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

This website

The Photo Studio app contains no analytics. Our website, magnificentmediaict.com, uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages, films and buttons are used, for example how many people start the film or click Download. Google sees your IP address (Google Analytics doesn't store it), your browser and device, your approximate location and the pages you view; it never receives your photos, your name or your email address. We use it only to improve the site, never for advertising, and keep the data for 14 months. It sets no advertising cookies, sets no cookies at all for visitors in the EU, UK and Switzerland, and turns off entirely when your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal. You can also opt out with Google's browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
