Questions

Good to know.

How does the free trial work?

Download the app and everything is unlocked for 7 days. No card needed. After the trial the free editor keeps working, with exports up to 2048 px; the Pro tools wait for your license key.

How do I unlock it after buying?

We email your license key. In the app, click any "Unlock with Pro" button, paste the key and click Activate. It works offline.

How many Macs can I use it on?

One license covers 2 of your Macs, for example a desktop and a laptop. Enter the same key on each.

Are updates free?

Every version 1 update (1.1, 1.2, 1.3 and so on) is free, and the app tells you when one is out. When version 2.0 arrives, license holders can upgrade for $25. Your license never expires.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Every edit and every AI feature runs on your Mac. Your originals are never changed. See the privacy policy.

What do I need?

A Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or later. HDR export needs macOS 15 or later.

Is there a subscription version?

Yes. Photo Studio is also coming to the Mac App Store with a monthly or yearly Photo Studio Pro plan. A website license unlocks only the website download, and an App Store subscription unlocks only the App Store app.

Can I get help?

Email hello@magnificentmediaict.com with "Photo Studio" in the subject. We reply within one business day. The support page has shortcuts and answers.
