Getting started

  • Drag photos or a whole folder into the window, or choose File › Import Photos.
  • Tap Enhance for a one-tap starting point. Every change it makes is listed in plain English and can be adjusted.
  • Try a Look: hover to preview, click to apply, and set its strength. Looks sit on top of your own edits.
  • Shooting a set? Select the photos, click your best one and press ⇧⌘M: every photo takes its look, with brightness and color matched one by one.
  • Choose Export to save finished photos, or tick Social Pack for 1:1, 4:5 and 9:16 versions framed on faces. Adding your logo is optional and it never covers a face.
  • New here? Help › Photo Studio Tour takes a minute, and Help › Open Sample Photos gives you seven photos to practice on.

Editing inside Apple Photos

  • Open a photo in Photos, click Edit, then the ⋯ button, and choose Photo Studio. Your edit is saved with the photo, so you can reopen it and keep adjusting later.
  • If Photo Studio isn't in the list, choose ⋯ › More… (or System Settings › General › Login Items & Extensions › Photos Editing) and switch it on.
  • Editing inside Photos is part of Photo Studio Pro (including the free trial). Open Photo Studio once after unlocking so Photos knows.

Requirements and formats

  • macOS 14 Sonoma or later. HDR export needs macOS 15 or later.
  • In English and Spanish: Photo Studio follows your Mac's language.
  • Opens camera RAW files supported by macOS (Canon, Nikon, Sony, Fujifilm, DNG and more), plus HEIC, JPEG, PNG and TIFF.
  • Exports JPEG, HEIC, TIFF (16-bit) and PNG in sRGB or Display P3. RAW files and iPhone HDR photos can be saved as HDR HEIC.

Questions

Are my originals changed? Never. Edits are saved as small recipes and applied fresh each time you view or export.

Where are my edits stored? In the app's own storage on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded.

Can I share my looks? Yes. Right-click a look and choose "Export as .mmlook", then import it on another Mac with the import button in the Looks panel.

Can I use my own LUTs? Yes. Load any 3D .cube file in the Looks panel and set its amount.

Why is the Sky mask unavailable? It turns on only when the app's on-device scene check finds sky, so it never paints a white ceiling or wall by mistake.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription? Subscriptions bought in the Mac App Store are managed in your App Store account settings (App Store › your name › Account Settings › Subscriptions). To restore a subscription on a new Mac, click any Unlock with Pro button, then Restore Purchases.
